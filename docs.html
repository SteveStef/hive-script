Everything a bee can do

Your colony runs two scripts. forager.bee runs on every worker bee, and queen.bee runs on the queen, who never leaves the hive. Each card below shows a function, which script can use it, and how much game time it takes.

The clips are the real game running the example script beside them, on a loop at 2× speed. The highlighted line is the one the bee is running, and anything it prints appears under the clip. Click a clip to pause it.

Positions are [x, y] tiles, with [0, 0] at the top-left. The hive is at [2, 6]. A tick is the game's unit of time; 1× runs 15 ticks a second.
